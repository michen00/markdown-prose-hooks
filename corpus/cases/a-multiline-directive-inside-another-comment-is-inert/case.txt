name: A multi-line directive inside another comment is not a directive
why: The outer comment opens first and everything to its closing delimiter is its content, so the inner opener is part of what the outer comment carries and the content is not the bare marker. One comment nested in another is a note about the form rather than a use of it, and the exact match on the whole content is what tells them apart.
