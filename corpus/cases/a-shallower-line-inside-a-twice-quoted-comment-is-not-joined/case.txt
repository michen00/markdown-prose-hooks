name: Lines inside a twice-quoted comment pass through even where the quote is shallower
why: A comment that opens two levels deep is a comment run, and every still-quoted line under it belongs to that run until the closing delimiter, which is already how the one-level shape behaves. So the two middle lines go back as they arrived rather than folding into a quoted paragraph of their own. Their depth is not the comment's, which is the second thing pinned here: the marker they spell is read only when the whole comment carries one prefix, so this one arms nothing and the paragraph below joins.
