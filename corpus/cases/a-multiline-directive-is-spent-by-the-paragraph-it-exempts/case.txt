name: A multi-line directive covers one paragraph and is then spent
why: The extent of the directive is the whole comment, and what it governs is the one paragraph after it -- not the rest of the file. An implementation arming at the closing line could just as easily leave the flag set, and the symptom would be silent: a document that stopped being processed from its first multi-line comment onward. Running the tool on this output has to change nothing further, which is the property a formatter most needs and the one an exempt paragraph sitting beside a joined one is most likely to break.
paragraphs_unwrapped: 1
line_breaks_removed: 1
